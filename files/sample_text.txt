The Quiet Server Room

Every morning before the office opens, Olivia walks past the server room.
The machines hum quietly behind the glass. She likes to stop at the small
café on the ground floor, order a flat white, and read the overnight
reports while the building wakes up.

Most reports are dull, and dull is good. A dull report means the backups
finished, the disks have space, and nobody tried to guess a password at
two in the morning. Olivia has learned that the interesting days are the
ones to worry about.

Today the report is long. One address asked for the same page again and
again, far more often than any person could. Olivia does not panic. She
counts, she compares, and she writes down exactly what she sees. Good
analysis begins with careful counting, and careful counting begins with
clean data.

By nine the office is busy, and the puzzle is solved. It was only a
faulty script, written by a tired colleague. Olivia smiles, saves her
notes, and finishes her coffee.
