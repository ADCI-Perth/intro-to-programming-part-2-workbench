title: Learner Profiles
---

This course is for people who have finished Part 1, mostly working adults who want to move into data work or cyber security. These profiles are examples, not real people.

## IT support officer moving towards cyber security

Olivia, 34, started as an administration officer and now works in IT support in Perth. She finished Part 1 and wants to move into a security analyst role. She reads logs by eye every day and wants to write small programs that count and summarise them. The weeks on log files, hashing and checking input are where the course pays off for her. She also appreciates that the security content is about checking and reporting, not attacking.

## Finance clerk who wants to automate reports

Noah, 45, works in accounts and spends hours each month copying figures between spreadsheets. After Part 1 he wants to read files, tidy data and draw charts with code, and eventually move into a data analyst role. Weeks 2, 5 and 6 (files, Pandas and charts) are where the course pays off for him. The Week 5 bridge on cleaning data and personal information matters to him because he handles customer records at work.

## Customer service team leader changing career

Emma, 29, leads a customer service team and finished Part 1 with some confidence. She is still nervous about maths and technical words. Part 2 explains every new word the first time it appears, and the statistics in Week 4 stay at everyday arithmetic with a worked example by hand. The Week 6 capstone lets her choose between a security dataset and a sales dataset, so she can pick the one closer to the job she wants.
